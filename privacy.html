Range Results Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version

Range Results does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your drills, runs, scores, times, notes and settings are saved in a file on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or your computer, that backup can include Range Results’s file, the same as other apps. That is handled by Apple, not by Range Results.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No servers: the app makes no network connections.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Backups and sharing

Settings › Back up all data saves your data (and a spreadsheet of your runs) through the iPhone share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, AirDrop, email and so on) is up to you. Restore from a backup reads a file you choose.

Once a day the app also keeps a backup copy on your iPhone, in the Files app under On My iPhone › Range Results. It stays on your iPhone.

Sharing a drill you built creates a link that contains only that drill’s layout and description, and you choose who to send it to.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase all data in the app, or delete the app from your iPhone. Both remove all of your Range Results data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
