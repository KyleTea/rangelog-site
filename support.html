You’ll need a shot timer

Range Results scores your runs but doesn’t time them. Shoot with your shot timer, then type its time into the app and tap in your hits.

Common questions

How is the hit factor worked out? Points divided by time. Alpha is 5 points; Charlie and Delta depend on your power factor (Minor: 3 and 1, Major: 4 and 2). Each miss is −10. See Settings › Help › How scoring works.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There’s no account. Use Settings › Back up all data to save a copy, and Restore from a backup to bring it back. The app also keeps a daily copy in the Files app under On My iPhone › Range Results.

How do I see the tutorial again? Settings › Help › Show tutorial.

Can I change a preset drill? Presets can’t be edited. Open one and tap Duplicate to make your own copy.
